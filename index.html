I am an AI/ML researcher and engineer focused on developing efficient, trustworthy, and multimodal AI systems, particularly at the intersection of medical imaging, clinical language, and foundation models. I completed my MS in Data Science at Riphah International University in April 2026, graduating with a 4.0/4.0 CGPA and first position in my cohort.

My broader interests span medical image analysis, clinical NLP, efficient foundation-model adaptation, and low-resource multilingual NLP.

Ethical Considerations of LLMs in Healthcare

First-author systematic review of 27 studies examining privacy, bias, safety, transparency, and accountability in healthcare LLMs. Proposes a four-layer ethical framework spanning regulatory safeguards, technical safeguards, human oversight, and accountability.

Background
