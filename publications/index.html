CXRG-SVLM: A Parameter-Efficient Small Vision-Language Model for Multi-View Chest X-ray Report Generation in Resource-Constrained Environments

Accepted · 2026

Muhammad Fareed, Jamal Uddin, A. H. Sayyid, Adeel Ahmed, Muhammad Awais Sattar

Scientific Reports — Accepted for publication; article DOI pending.

A parameter-efficient medical vision-language model combining multi-view chest X-ray analysis, a frozen RAD-DINO visual encoder, attention-based fusion, and QLoRA-adapted Qwen2.5-3B. The model has 38.11M trainable parameters (1.91%) and was evaluated on IU-Xray using complementary lexical, semantic, and radiology-specific metrics. External and radiologist-led validation have not yet been performed.

A Systematic Review of Ethical Considerations of Large Language Models in Healthcare and Medicine

Published · 2025

Muhammad Fareed, Madeeha Fatima, Jamal Uddin, Adeel Ahmed, Muhammad Awais Sattar

Frontiers in Digital Health, 7:1653631.

First-author systematic review synthesizing 27 studies from 316 screened records, examining privacy, bias, safety, transparency, accountability, and human oversight in healthcare LLMs. Proposes a four-layer framework encompassing regulatory safeguards, technical safeguards, human oversight, and accountability.
